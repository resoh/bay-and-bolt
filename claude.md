# Bay & Bolt — System Architecture & Operational Playbook

> **Status: Concept draft.** Everything below — hardware specs, the universal mounting system, pricing formulas, and margins — is a starting point for partner discussion, not a finalized design. Treat specific numbers and mechanisms as illustrative until validated against real vendor quotes, prototypes, and installer time studies.

## Executive Summary & Core Concept
* **High-Concept Pitch:** Modular, structural garage storage installations ranging from heavy-duty slat-wall and overhead racking to luxury, fully customized workshop cabinetry suites, paired with an optional full-service concierge garage reset.
* **Core Innovation:** Standardized structural hardware across all tiers anchored by a heavy-duty wall-mounting track and an overhead ceiling rack rated for 600+ lbs, enabling seamless client upgrades from Tier 1 to Tier 3 over time without wall damage.
* **Market Opportunity:** Bridges the gap between cheap wire/plastic garage shelving ($100–$350, buckles under bins and power tools) and hyper-expensive custom garage cabinetry systems ($5,000–$15,000+ with long lead times).
* **Target ICP:** Suburban homeowners with 2–3 car garages, DIY/hobbyist workshop owners, rental property managers, and luxury homeowners wanting a showroom-grade garage.
* **Core Guarantee:** "Built to hold what your garage floor can't"—a lifetime anti-collapse warranty on all structural framing.

## Hardware Sourcing & Standardization Architecture

### Sourcing Strategy Matrix
* **Tier 1 (Heavy-Duty Slat Wall & Overhead Rack):**
  * *Material:* Powder-coated steel or PVC slat-wall panels with heavy-duty hooks/baskets; a ceiling-mounted overhead storage rack rated to 600 lbs.
  * *Sourcing:* Direct commercial accounts with garage-storage OEM suppliers (e.g., Gladiator, NewAge, StoreWALL) or regional steel service centers. Pre-sized panels to eliminate job-site cutting.
  * *Anchoring:* Wall panels secured to studs via 1/4" structural lag screws; overhead rack secured to ceiling joists via rated lag bolts, load-tested before handoff.
* **Tier 2 (The Modular Cabinet System):**
  * *Material:* Powder-coated steel modular cabinets, adjustable shelving, pegboard tool-wall inserts.
  * *Sourcing:* Commercial OEM storage suppliers (e.g., NewAge Products, Gladiator, Husky) at 40–50% off retail.
* **Tier 3 (The Workshop Suite):**
  * *Substrates:* 3/4" Thermally Fused Laminate (TFL) over industrial-grade MDF (80% of builds to eliminate on-site painting/finishing), with a butcher-block or stainless workbench top.
  * *Sourcing:* 4'x8' sheets via regional architectural panel suppliers; workbench hardware (vise, power strip) via commercial tool distributor.

### Universal Mounting System (Upgrade Path)
* **Universal Slat-Wall Track:** 14-gauge cold-rolled steel continuous wall track mounted into every stud.
* **Tier 1 Attachment:** Slat-wall panels and overhead rack brackets lock directly into the universal track using quick-pin fasteners.
* **Tier 2 Attachment:** Modular steel cabinets and pegboard inserts snap directly onto the universal track.
* **Tier 3 Attachment:** Structural MDF cabinet carcasses and the workbench substructure slot into the exact same track system, converting the space into a full built-in workshop suite without tearing out original wall attachments.

## Financial Models & Pricing Formulas

### Financial Target Metrics
* **Target Gross Margin:** Minimum **65%** overall across all tiers and services.
* **Target Labor Cost:** **15% – 18%** of total job price (sub-contracted or employee installer flat-rate payouts).

### Base Retail Pricing Formula
$$\text{Linear Foot Retail Price} = \frac{\text{Direct Material Cost/ft} + \text{Direct Labor Cost/ft}}{1 - \text{Target Gross Margin Percent}}$$

## Premium Accessories & Upgrades (Tier 3 Upsell)

* **Positioning:** High-margin, sales-floor upsell layered onto a Tier 3 Workshop Suite quote. Priced and sold as discrete line items, not baked into the linear-foot rate.
* **Sourcing Model:** Fulfilled through certified third-party equipment manufacturers rather than in-house fabrication — Bay & Bolt handles carpentry/track integration and install; the manufacturer's own warranty covers the unit itself.

### Accessory Catalog

| Accessory | Target Retail Price | Est. Install Time | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heavy-Duty Workbench w/ Vise** | $600 – $1,600 | 2.0 – 3.0 Hours | Butcher-block or stainless top, integrated vise mount, power strip. |
| **Motorized Bike/Kayak Hoist** | $250 – $600 | 1.0 – 1.5 Hours | Ceiling-mounted pulley hoist system; rated per manufacturer spec to ceiling joists. |
| **Overhead Rack Expansion (600 lb)** | $300 – $700 | 1.5 – 2.0 Hours | Additional ceiling-mounted rack bay, same load rating as Tier 1 base rack. |
| **Pegboard Tool Wall Organizer** | $150 – $400 | 0.75 Hours | Add-on insert to any Tier 2/3 cabinet run. |
| **EV Charger-Ready Wall Prep** | $200 – $500 | 1.0 Hour | Conduit and mounting plate prep; final circuit run by licensed electrician, quoted separately. |
| **Epoxy Floor Coating** | $1,500 – $4,000 | Separate trade (1–2 days) | Fulfilled through a certified epoxy-flooring partner; scheduled alongside cabinetry install, not performed by Bay & Bolt installers. |

### Liability & Compliance Protocol
* **Not Covered by Structural Warranty:** Hoists, EV charger circuits, and epoxy flooring carry the manufacturer's or partner's own warranty; Bay & Bolt's Lifetime Structural Guarantee covers the surrounding cabinetry/track/overhead-rack framing only.
* **Electrical Disclosure:** Any accessory requiring a dedicated circuit (EV charger, powered hoist) requires licensed-electrician sign-off before install; not included in the accessory price.
* **Overhead Rack Load Testing:** Every overhead rack install is load-tested on-site before handoff and the client is shown the rated capacity in writing.
* **Sales Script Cue:** Position as *"We build the frame around it — the hoist, the charger, the floor all come from certified partners with their own warranty."*

## White-Glove Garage Reset Concierge Integration

### Package Breakdown
* **Basic Reset:** Systematically pack garage contents into totes, complete hardware installation, and restock items back by category.
* **Concierge Reset:** Full inventorying, donation/haul-away purge support, professional zone-based organization (tools, sports gear, seasonal decor, automotive), labeled bins and hooks, and old packaging/box removal.

### Two-Person Operational Protocol


[Arrival & Digital Pre-Audit] (Installer + Organizer)
│
▼
[Pack-Down & Staging] (Organizer Lead)
└── Mobile totes and a driveway staging area outside the work zone
│
▼
[Tear-Out & Structural Hardware Build] (Installer Lead)
└── Remove old shelving, mount slat-wall track and overhead rack, assemble Tier 1-3 modules, cleanup
│
▼
[Restock & Zone Organization] (Organizer Lead)
└── Labeled bins and hooks by zone — tools, sports gear, seasonal, automotive

### Risk & Liability Protocols
* **Hazardous Materials Disclosure:** Organizer flags fuel, propane, chemicals, and other hazardous materials for client handling — these are never packed, moved, or stored by Bay & Bolt staff.
* **Pre-Existing Damage Audit:** High-resolution digital photo log taken of all existing garage conditions and stored items prior to touching contents.

## Digital Quote Engine & Lead Conversion Architecture

### Calculator Logic Workflow
1. **User Inputs:** Wall length (linear feet), garage size (1/2/3-car), and current photos.
2. **Hardware Selection:** User toggles between Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 pricing.
3. **Service Add-On:** Dynamic checkbox for **Basic Reset** ($32/ft, min $320) or **Concierge Reset** ($70/ft, min $700).
4. **Formula Execution:** `Total Quote = (Selected Tier $/ft * Linear Ft) + MAX(Service Floor Price, Service $/ft * Linear Ft)`
5. **Output:** Instant 3D parametric preview + 3-tiered price matrix + $99 refundable deposit booking link.

## Lifetime Warranty Structure

* **Lifetime Structural Guarantee:** Covers wall anchoring integrity, slat-wall/track failure, overhead rack anchoring, and structural joinery for homeownership duration.
* **5-Year Mechanical Warranty:** Covers soft-close drawer slides, hinges, workbench vise mount, and edge-banding.
* **Weight Limits:** 150 lbs/linear foot for Tier 1 wall track; 120 lbs/linear foot for Tiers 2 & 3; overhead rack rated to 600 lbs total load, independently load-tested per install.
